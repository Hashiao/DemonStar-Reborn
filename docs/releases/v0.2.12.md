# v0.2.12 — 分类设置、暂停难度与三色火力 / Settings, live difficulty and retained power

设置现在分为声音与震动、玩家与操作、画面与界面、游戏规则、语言。单人、同屏双人及局域网双人的开局流程均先选择从头开始或关卡，再选择难度。读档可从开局入口或游戏暂停菜单进入，存档不再混在设置中。

暂停时可以调整难度并继续当前战斗，不重置生命、装备、分数、敌人或弹幕；联机由房主统一调整。存档仍保存关卡入口状态，读档从该关开头恢复入口难度、装备和进度，兼容旧档。

新增默认关闭的“三色火力进度保留”：黄、蓝、红分别累积至 6 级，换色和低能量不降级，只有该玩家死亡才清空。黄 6 → 蓝 1 后再吃黄球，会切回黄 6 并触发一次黄色清屏；之后再吃蓝球升为蓝 2。S 只补满当前颜色。此规则开局确定、读档恢复，并随联机快照同步；它是可选扩展，不是原作规则。联机双方需更新到兼容版本。

保留原作红 P1 / 蓝 P2、飞机余命图标、逐枚分类炸弹及 16 格能量条。实际游戏仍以单人/双人为限，3P/4P 只保留协议与核心扩展能力。敌机基础 HP、玩家伤害表、超级武器预算及原版十八关编排不变。

Settings now separate sound/haptics, player controls, display/interface, game rules and language. Solo, same-device two-player and LAN two-player setup all offer a fresh start or stage selection followed by difficulty. Load saves from setup or pause; saves are no longer buried in settings.

Change difficulty while paused and continue the same battle without resetting lives, equipment, score, enemies or projectiles. LAN hosts control the shared difficulty. Saves remain stage-entry checkpoints: loading restores the entry difficulty, equipment and progress at the beginning of that stage. Legacy saves remain compatible.

The optional, default-off retained-power rule tracks yellow, blue and red independently up to level 6. Color changes and low energy do not downgrade them; only their owner's death clears them. After yellow 6 → blue 1, another yellow pickup equips yellow 6 and triggers one yellow nova; the next blue pickup gives blue 2. S fills only the equipped color. The rule is selected before launch, restored by loading and replicated over LAN. It is an optional extension, not an original mechanic. Both LAN peers need compatible versions.

Original red P1 / blue P2, spare-ship icons, individually typed bombs and sixteen energy segments remain. Public play is still solo/two-player; P3/P4 remain protocol/core reserves. Enemy base HP, player damage tables, superweapon budgets and the original eighteen-stage campaign are unchanged.

176 engine/unit tests, 19 browser suites and Android build/Lint/API 37 checks passed (12 Lint warnings per variant, no errors). [iOS CI](https://github.com/Hashiao/DemonStar-Reborn/actions/runs/38092413740) passed iPhoneOS compilation, iOS 18.5 iPhone/iPad localization/native music, simulator-pair TCP difficulty/color replication and reconnection, and XCTest system long presses. The IPA is unsigned and requires user signing before installation. Deployment targets iOS 12 / Android 10 do not establish physical-device testing on those versions. Physical hotspots, Android–iOS hardware pairs, physical controllers and haptic feel remain untested.
