# v0.2.7 · 出击、气罐与蓝色激光修复 / Launch, tanker and blue-laser fixes

## 简体中文

- 母舰出击含舱门从约8秒缩短到约3.7秒，位移插值保持流畅；战斗速度和计时不加速。
- 首关大煤气罐使用原作44×87可见比例，修正整格拉伸导致的瘦长外形，不改700HP或碰撞尺寸。
- 普通敌机与 Boss 血条分成独立开关。**默认仅开启 Boss 血条**；普通条开启后只显示最大血量不低于开局方形补给机（905HP）的敌人。
- 蓝色炸弹改为紫色闪电罩包住主角、粗蓝激光连接前方目标，跟随移动并停在最近的可攻击敌人处；不再发射护罩圆环。
- 保留三语、独立设置保存及其他已完成还原。旧合并血条设置一次性迁移为 Boss-only，此后尊重两项独立选择。

蓝炮保留旧4秒/每4步720的伤害预算；未改基础HP和普通武器表。母舰墙钟时长、高清效果及碰撞仍有校准/近似，未宣称完整1:1。[证据与边界](https://github.com/Hashiao/DemonStar-Reborn/blob/main/docs/M2_7_RESEARCH.md) · [素材与提示词](https://github.com/Hashiao/DemonStar-Reborn/blob/main/docs/ART_M2_7.md)。

## English

- Carrier launch including doors is shortened from about8s to3.7s with smooth interpolation. Combat speed and clocks are unchanged.
- The large stage-one tanker now uses its original44×87 visible proportions instead of stretching the padded atlas cell. Its700HP and collision geometry remain unchanged.
- Regular and boss health bars have independent settings. **Only boss bars are enabled by default.** Regular bars, when enabled, require at least the opening supply ship's905 maximum HP.
- The blue bomb now surrounds the fighter with violet lightning and projects a thick blue beam toward the nearest eligible target. Both follow the player; the aura is no longer launched as travelling rings.
- Trilingual UI, persistent settings and existing fidelity fixes remain. Old combined bar settings migrate once to Boss-only; subsequent independent choices are respected.

The blue weapon retains the previous4-second/720-per-four-tick budget. Base HP and ordinary weapon tables are unchanged. Launch timing, HD effects and collision still include calibration/approximations; complete1:1 fidelity is not claimed. [Evidence and limits](https://github.com/Hashiao/DemonStar-Reborn/blob/main/docs/M2_7_RESEARCH.md) · [Art and prompt](https://github.com/Hashiao/DemonStar-Reborn/blob/main/docs/ART_M2_7.md).

Android10/API29、iOS12为部署目标，最低系统与真机未实测。IPA为 **unsigned**，需自行签名。

Deployment targets are Android10/API29 and iOS12; minimum versions and physical devices are untested. The IPA is **unsigned** and requires your own signing.
