# v0.2.6 · 三语界面与双语维护 / Trilingual UI and bilingual maintenance

## 简体中文

- 菜单、设置、HUD、帮助、补给提示、Boss 名称、结算和无障碍文案支持简体中文、繁体中文、英语。
- 首次启动按原生首选语言识别：明确 Hans/Hant 字形优先；台湾/香港/澳门中文用繁体，其他中文用简体，其他语言用英语。
- 主菜单及暂停设置可立即切换，保存选择；重启或改变系统语言后优先保留手动选择。旧存档保留分数、解锁、难度和声音设置，切换不重开战斗。
- 繁体文案独立润色，使用「設定、主選單、搖桿、飛彈、電漿砲、強化火力」等通用游戏表达，不只是转换字形。
- 桌面名称随系统资源语言显示，原作标志和英文无线电保留。玩法、数值和已完成的还原修复未改变。
- 长期双语要求写入项目规范；核心注释、README、重要提交及发布信息提供简体中文和英文。

部署目标 Android10/API29、iOS12。IPA 为 **unsigned** 设备包，需自行签名。最低系统及真机未实测。繁体版不声称经过地区母语玩家审校。

## English

- Added Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and English to menus, settings, HUD, help, pickup feedback, boss names, results and accessibility labels.
- First launch uses the native preferred language. Explicit Hans/Hant scripts take precedence; Taiwan/Hong Kong/Macao Chinese selects Traditional, other Chinese selects Simplified, and other languages fall back to English.
- Switch instantly in main-menu or pause settings. Choices survive restarts and later system-language changes. Existing scores, unlocks, difficulty and audio settings are preserved, and switching does not restart combat.
- Traditional Chinese is independently worded with familiar game terms such as 設定, 主選單, 搖桿, 飛彈, 電漿砲 and 強化火力, rather than automatic character conversion.
- Launcher names follow system resources. The original logo and English radio remain. Gameplay, numeric rules and existing fidelity fixes are unchanged.
- The permanent bilingual policy is documented. Core comments, READMEs, significant commits and release information are available in Simplified Chinese and English.

Targets remain Android10/API29 and iOS12. The device IPA is **unsigned** and requires your own signing. Minimum OS versions and physical devices are untested. Regional native-speaker review of Traditional Chinese is not claimed.
