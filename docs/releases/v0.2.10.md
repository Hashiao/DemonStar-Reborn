# M2.10 · 双人操作、局域网与原作界面

版本 0.2.10（12），Android 10/API 29+、iOS 12+。当前开放单人和双人；3P/4P 仅协议与内核预留，菜单、同机模式和局域网房间均不开放四人玩法。

- 同机双人分别拥有摇杆/A/B，支持同时移动、开火和投弹。加入可调整位置/大小的固定摇杆、随按下点建立中心的浮动摇杆、八方向按键，以及键盘、鼠标、手柄分配和简单改键。
- 原生局域网采用房主/加入、地址和六位房间码。两台设备各控制一人，目标配对为 Android–Android、Android–iOS、iOS–iOS；先由系统连接同一 Wi-Fi/热点。房主决定开始、继续、切关和读档；任何玩家可暂停，断线暂停并保留座位和装备重连。
- 保留已确认的经典 HUD：红色 P1、蓝色 P2，余命小飞机、蓝色能量格和逐枚炸弹。P2 状态组在右下角，小蓝机在能量条左端，炸弹在上方。
- 设置/暂停可选原版 1–18 关；自动档与三个手动槽位保存关卡起点的生命、装备和得分，加载从该关开始。不是逐帧中途续存。
- 两端原生受伤/投弹/死亡/拾取触感，支持开关、能力检测与节流。冷启动主菜单尝试播放 BGM，并尊重静音、零音量和后台状态。
- Boss 按原作标志区分原位爆炸与金标中的侧上坠毁，修正全部机型统一下移/侧翻；结算恢复战场叠层、玩家奖励框和图标三行。Android/iOS 统一使用原作战机参考重绘的像素图标。

原作 HP、伤害表、18 关数据、显式原型/敌弹映射及五类固定底座炮台保留。三语切换继续即时生效且不重置战斗。

## 验收与边界

发布验收进行中；正式发布前须补齐匹配源码的双端结果和 SHA-256。

自动化证明的范围与真机分开记录。浏览器模拟多指/手柄不等于真实触屏/外设认证；模拟器原生 TCP 不等于实际手机热点互通。最低 Android 10/iOS 12 系统、跨品牌热点、实体手柄和振动手感仍未实测。九种受损贴图已补齐，碎片和火焰细节、字体及转场节奏仍有差异，不宣称完整 1:1。

APK 使用本项目独立签名。IPA 文件名为 `DemonStar.ipa`，未签名，必须自行签名后使用；不能直接安装。浏览器可玩单人/同机双人，原生局域网需要安装版。

# M2.10 · Two-player controls, LAN and classic presentation

Version 0.2.10 (12), targeting Android 10/API 29+ and iOS 12+. Solo and two-player play are exposed. P3/P4 remain protocol/core reserves; no four-player menu, local mode or public room is available.

- Two local players have separate movement/A/B controls for simultaneous movement, shooting and bombs. Controls include adjustable fixed sticks, touch-centered floating sticks, eight-way pads, keyboard/mouse/controller assignment and simple remapping.
- Native LAN provides host/join, an address and six-digit room code. Two devices control one player each, targeting Android–Android, Android–iOS and iOS–iOS on a system-established Wi-Fi/hotspot network. The host controls launch/resume/stage/load; either player can pause. Disconnect pauses the room and reconnect retains seats/equipment.
- Preserve the approved classic HUD: red P1, blue P2, spare-ship icons, blue energy segments and individual bombs. P2 sits bottom-right, with its blue ship left of the energy bar and bombs above.
- Settings/pause allow all eighteen original stages. Automatic and three manual slots retain stage-entry lives/equipment/scores; loading restarts the stage rather than resuming an arbitrary battle frame.
- Native haptics follow hits, bombs, deaths and pickups with preferences, capability checks and rate limiting. Cold-menu BGM attempts playback while respecting mute, zero volume and background state.
- Original flags now distinguish in-place Boss explosions from the reference's sideways/upward death animation, replacing universal translation/rolling. Stage results use battlefield overlays, player panels and three icon reward rows. Android/iOS share a newly redrawn pixel launcher icon based on the original fighter.

Original HP/damage tables, eighteen-stage data, explicit art/projectile mappings and five fixed-base turret families are preserved. Language changes remain immediate without combat resets.

## Verification and limits

Release verification is in progress; matching native results and SHA-256 checksums must be completed before publication.

Report automation separately from hardware testing. Browser touch/controller fixtures do not certify physical devices, and simulator TCP does not certify actual phone hotspots. Minimum Android 10/iOS 12 systems, cross-vendor hotspots, physical controllers and haptic feel remain untested. Nine damaged hull redraws are included; debris, flames, fonts and transition timing still differ; complete 1:1 fidelity is not claimed.

The APK uses this project's independent signing key. The ASCII-named `DemonStar.ipa` is unsigned and requires user signing; it is not directly installable. Browsers support solo/local two-player play, while native LAN requires an installed app.

See the linked development, network and presentation records above for implementation evidence and asset provenance.
