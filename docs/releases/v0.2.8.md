# v0.2.8 · 追踪导弹与三色六档 / Homing missiles and weapon tiers

## 简体中文

- 追踪弹从 8×17 恢复为原作细小的 4×8；定速飞行、循环分配目标并保持锁定，目标失效才重选，修正转弯逐渐减速和频繁换目标。
- 恢复原作转向步长及转后对齐、16 向姿态、101 步自毁和发射初期位移继承。
- 黄色高档侧弹恢复并排成对轮廓；蓝色按三种长度组合六档；红色按六档加宽，辅助小弹恢复独立外形及减速过程。去掉此前红蓝额外 1.5 倍放大。
- 保留三语和默认仅 Boss 血条。基础 HP、主机伤害表、主炮射速与超级武器预算未修改。
- 保存用户提供的十八关录像为长期参考，并提供三色六档的实际渲染对比图。

[原作证据与剩余边界](https://github.com/Hashiao/DemonStar-Reborn/blob/main/docs/M2_8_RESEARCH.md) · [录像金标及抽样时刻](https://github.com/Hashiao/DemonStar-Reborn/blob/main/docs/REFERENCE_VIDEO.md) · [内置 imagegen 素材与完整提示词](https://github.com/Hashiao/DemonStar-Reborn/blob/main/docs/ART_M2_8.md)。目标槽位回收、碰撞和烟迹仍有近似，磁力转向未在本轮核验，未声称完整 1:1。

## English

- Homing missiles return from 8×17 to the original thin 4×8 silhouette. They fly at constant speed, cycle initial targets and hold a lock until invalid, fixing speed loss while turning and frequent target switching.
- Restore source turn steps and post-turn alignment, sixteen headings, a 101-tick fuse and initial player-motion inheritance.
- Yellow side shots regain their paired silhouettes; three blue lengths compose six tiers; red shafts widen through six tiers with separate decelerating auxiliary pellets. Remove the previous extra 1.5 enlargement of red/blue sprites.
- Preserve three languages and Boss-only health-bar defaults. Base HP, player damage tables, main firing cadence and superweapon budgets remain unchanged.
- Retain the user's eighteen-stage recording as a long-term reference and provide actual renders of all color/tier combinations.

[Evidence and remaining limits](https://github.com/Hashiao/DemonStar-Reborn/blob/main/docs/M2_8_RESEARCH.md) · [Recording and reviewed timestamps](https://github.com/Hashiao/DemonStar-Reborn/blob/main/docs/REFERENCE_VIDEO.md) · [Built-in imagegen asset and full prompts](https://github.com/Hashiao/DemonStar-Reborn/blob/main/docs/ART_M2_8.md). Target-slot reuse order, collision and trails remain approximate; magnetic steering is outside this verification. Complete 1:1 fidelity is not claimed.

Android 10/API29、iOS 12 是部署目标；最低系统及真机未实测。IPA **未签名，需要自行签名**。

Android 10/API29 and iOS 12 are deployment targets; minimum versions and physical devices remain untested. The IPA is **unsigned and requires your own signing**.
