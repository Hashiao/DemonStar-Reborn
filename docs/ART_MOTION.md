# 主机侧身与喷焰图集

输出：`web/assets/player-motion-hd.png`。使用内置 imagegen，参考本机原作 PLAYER1A～Q 与 PLCHFLMA；原图仅保存在忽略目录。17 个侧身姿态与 3 个喷焰形态，5 列 × 4 行，透明背景；姿态与尾焰由代码分别控制。

实际提示词：

Use case: style-transfer. One production transparent sprite atlas for the faithful DemonStar HD remake. Input image 1 is the authoritative LOCAL ORIGINAL player-bank sprite reference with 17 poses PLAYER1A through PLAYER1Q. Input image 2 is the original exhaust flame color/shape reference. Redraw, do not redesign. Output one 5-column by 4-row equal-cell atlas (20 cells), each object precisely centered, true transparent RGBA background, no labels, borders, shadows, text, grid or particles outside each sprite. First 17 cells in ROW-MAJOR order must be the exact same RED player aircraft in 17 incremental roll/banking poses from A through Q: A strongly banked LEFT, B through H gradually approach level, I (cell index8, row2 col4) exactly level top-down symmetrical, J through P gradually bank RIGHT, Q strongly banked RIGHT. Aircraft always points straight UP; banking is roll around its longitudinal nose-tail axis, NEVER yaw/spinning in the picture plane. Preserve the original narrow red fuselage, long pointed pale nose with orange/yellow cockpit panel, red swept delta wings, silver-gray wing rims and separate silver elongated side cannon rails; no extra wings, blue cockpit, engine pods or added decorations. Under strong bank the raised wing becomes foreshortened and fuselage side is visible, original outline must remain immediately recognizable. All aircraft have the SAME longitudinal size at every angle, center and nose aligned in each cell, fit within 70 percent cell width/height. Crisp high-resolution late-1990s pre-rendered metal style with restrained highlights. No exhaust baked into aircraft cells. Final 3 cells (indices17,18,19) contain isolated single vertical exhaust sprites pointing DOWN: short fluttering idle flame, long accelerating jet flame, short wide braking flame. Yellow-white hot core, orange edges and reddish tapered tail exactly like original reference 2; never blue. Each exhaust centered in its own cell, narrow shape about 22 percent cell width and 65 percent height. Transparency throughout. One consistent airframe and orthographic camera, exact orderly atlas essential.
